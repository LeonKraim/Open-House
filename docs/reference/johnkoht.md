## No claim, not even a stated one

This repository ships no licence file and its README makes no licence claim
either. Where `fwartner` leaves an intent to point at, this one leaves nothing:
the default applies without any competing statement, and the work is all rights
reserved by the author.

There is consequently no `readme_licence_claim` and no
`licence_claim_discrepancy` on the record. A discrepancy field records the gap
between a claim and a file; with no claim there is no gap to record, and
inventing one would overstate what the repository says.

## What may be reused

- **Ideas, and only ideas.** The repository is read for concepts and restated in
  our own words.
- **Identifiers.** As with every repo, entity identifiers are recorded facts
  about a naming scheme rather than authored text, so they are committed by the
  extraction and the hardcoding audit can run over this repo. The licence rule
  does not reach them, so this is not an exception to it.

## What may not be reused

- **Expression of any kind.** No alias, display name, comment, block of YAML or
  other authored text may reach a shipped artifact.
- **Prose.** No passage may be reproduced anywhere, `docs/` included. Ideas are
  described in our own words, as above.
- Behaviour taken from this repo carries a `concept` in our own words and an
  empty `expression`.

## Vendored third-party carve-out

Nothing in this repository is in the corpus for reuse, so the carve-out does not
change what is permitted here. Stated for completeness: the repository's
blueprints are third-party material namespaced by their own authors, are under
those authors' licences rather than this one's, are excluded by the
vendored-tree rules, and would not be attributed to this author even if the
repository carried a licence.
